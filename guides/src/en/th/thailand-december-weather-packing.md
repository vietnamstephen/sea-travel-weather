# Thailand in December: hot Bangkok, cool Chiang Mai nights, drier Phuket

This guide is from Yetu SEA Travel Weather. In December, one bag of short sleeves will not cover all of Thailand: its three most popular cities feel quite different.

## December climate averages (Thai Meteorological Department)

| City (station) | Mean max | Mean min | Rain days | Rainfall | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bangkok (Bangkok Metropolis) | 32.3°C | 23.2°C | 1.5 | 11.6 mm | [Thai Meteorological Department (TMD), Climatological Data 1991–2020, PDF p. 57 (printed p. 51)](https://ubonmet.tmd.go.th/files/MetInfo/climate_normal.pdf) |
| Chiang Mai (Chiang Mai) | 29.2°C | 16.9°C | 1.8 | 14.1 mm | [TMD, Climatological Data 1991–2020, PDF p. 11 (printed p. 5)](https://ubonmet.tmd.go.th/files/MetInfo/climate_normal.pdf) |
| Phuket (Phuket) | 32.1°C | 25.0°C | 10.3 | 82.0 mm | [TMD, Climatological Data 1991–2020, PDF p. 88 (printed p. 82)](https://ubonmet.tmd.go.th/files/MetInfo/climate_normal.pdf) |

Period: 1991–2020 averages (as labelled by TMD). Rain days follow TMD’s “mean rainy day” definition. Averages describe typical conditions, not a forecast for any given day.

## What December means in Thailand

The Thai Meteorological Department defines mid-October to mid-February as the winter (northeast monsoon) season, with fairly cool weather in December and January in upper Thailand — while the southern east coast, on the Gulf side, stays rainy into October–November ([source](https://www.tmd.go.th/en/climate/climate-of-thailand)).

The three cities still differ: Bangkok averages a 32.3°C high, Chiang Mai a 16.9°C low, and Phuket still sees about 10 rain days. Pack in layers by city.

## Bangkok: cool season, still hot by day

Bangkok’s average December high is 32.3°C with almost no rain (1.5 rain days). Short sleeves, light trousers or a skirt, a sun shirt and a hat stay in the core list; one light shirt or jacket is enough for malls and air-conditioned trains.

## Chiang Mai: short sleeves by day, a layer at night

Chiang Mai averages 29.2°C by day and 16.9°C at night in December — a swing of about 12°C. The lowest December reading in 1991–2020 was 3.8°C (the extreme minimum in the same TMD table). Short sleeves work for temples and cafés; sunrise, the night market and the hills call for long sleeves or a light jacket.

(Agricultural burning and air quality in the north are a later dry-season issue and are not covered here; check Thailand’s official air-quality information before you go.)

## Phuket: fewer rain days, not zero

Phuket averages 10.3 rain days in December, down from 22.7 in October (same TMD table) — less than half, but not dry. Pack sunscreen, swimwear and water-friendly shoes, plus a light layer for air-conditioning and evening breeze. Check sea conditions and the operator’s notice before boat trips.

Gulf islands such as Samui and Tao follow a different rain pattern, so do not reuse Phuket’s numbers for them.

## A multi-city packing list

1. Four short-sleeve shirts and two pairs of light trousers (Bangkok and Phuket by day).
2. One or two long-sleeve shirts and a light jacket (Chiang Mai mornings and nights; air-conditioning).
3. Swimwear and a full sun kit (hat, sunglasses, high-SPF sunscreen).
4. Walking shoes and sandals; a compact umbrella or light rain shell for Phuket.
5. A few days before you leave, compare the 7-day forecasts and weather-based outfit suggestions for Bangkok, Chiang Mai and Phuket on Yetu, then decide whether the jacket goes in your cabin bag.
