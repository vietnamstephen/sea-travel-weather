# Vietnam in December: cool Hanoi, hot south — what to pack

This guide is from Yetu SEA Travel Weather. Vietnam is long from north to south, so **there is no single “December weather.”** If your trip includes both Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, pack for two seasons.

## The north: Hanoi and Ha Long — cool and misty, bring a layer

Vietnam’s national tourism authority describes Hanoi and Ha Long from December to March as cool to cold, misty and cloudy, with Hanoi lows around 10°C from late December to March and cold January days on Ha Long Bay ([source](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/weather-and-climate-vietnam/)). The table puts Hanoi’s average December low at 15.4°C; cold spells can push it lower.

**What to wear:** long sleeves or a light sweater plus a windproof layer or light down jacket for mornings, evenings and outdoor queues; long trousers; closed walking shoes for damp, misty streets.

Mountain areas such as Sapa are colder than Hanoi — see our separate guide on Sapa and Chiang Mai in winter.

## The south: Ho Chi Minh City and Phu Quoc — dry season, hot and sunny

The tourism authority describes December to April around Ho Chi Minh City as clear, hot and sunny, and Phu Quoc as mostly fair and hot from October to June, with December and January a good time for the island ([source](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/weather-and-climate-vietnam/)). HCMC’s average December high is 31.6°C, and Phu Quoc averages about 6 rain days in December (QCVN 02:2022/BXD).

**What to wear:** breathable short sleeves, shorts or light trousers, a sun shirt, hat, sunglasses and strong sunscreen; swimwear and water-friendly sandals for the islands. One light layer is enough for air-conditioning.

## The centre: Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue — still many rain days

Da Nang averages 21 rain days and 272 mm of rain in December — the wettest city in the table. The tourism authority describes Hue as wet and cool from September to February, with occasional floods from September to November, and Hoi An as rainy from September to January ([source](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/weather-and-climate-vietnam/)). Do not pack for the centre as if it were the dry-season south.

**What to wear:** a packable rain shell and quick-dry shoes, plus short sleeves for sunny, hot spells. See our Da Nang and Hoi An guide for itinerary tips.

## One packing list for north and south

1. Three or four short-sleeve shirts (south, indoors).
2. Two long-sleeve shirts plus a light sweater or light down jacket (Hanoi, Ha Long, mornings).
3. Two pairs of trousers, one or two pairs of shorts or a skirt.
4. One packable rain shell (central coast, showers).
5. Walking shoes and sandals; sun protection and rain gear.
6. A few days before you leave, check the 7-day forecasts and weather-based outfit suggestions for Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang and Phu Quoc on Yetu, then decide whether the warm layer goes in your cabin bag or checked luggage.
