# Da Nang and Hoi An in December: can you still go in the rain?

## The short answer

This guide is from Yetu SEA Travel Weather. **Yes — but do not plan it like a dry-season beach trip.** Vietnam’s north, centre and south do not share one rainy season, and the central coast gets its rain later in the year.

Da Nang averages 21 rain days in December and Hue about 20, against 5 in Ho Chi Minh City — that is the main difference between the centre and the south.

## How the official tourism guide describes it

Vietnam’s national tourism authority says ([source](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/weather-and-climate-vietnam/)):

- Hue has a late rainy season, wet and cool from September to February, with occasional flooding from September to November.
- Hoi An is rainy and cool from September to January, with a lot of rain and some small floods from September to November.

So December is usually past the September–November flood period, but grey, wet days are still common and boat trips can be cancelled at short notice.

## A more resilient itinerary

1. Put outdoor and beach time in the clearer forecast windows and keep a spare day; do not stack boat trips back to back.
2. Give Hoi An Ancient Town a rain plan: a museum, a long lunch or a workshop.
3. If you combine Hue and Hoi An, leave extra travel time and drop a sight rather than the buffer.
4. Allow extra time from Da Nang airport to Hoi An in heavy rain.
5. Treat boat and island trips as changeable and check cancellation rules in advance.

## What to wear and carry

- A packable rain shell beats an umbrella on a windy shore.
- Quick-dry shoes or spare sandals.
- Da Nang’s average December high is about 25°C, so short sleeves and sunscreen still belong in the bag; add a light long-sleeve layer for evenings.
- A dry pouch for electronics.
